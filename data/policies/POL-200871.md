# Homeowners Policy

Policy Number: POL-200871
Policy Type: home
Policyholder: James Whitfield
Coverage Period: 2026-03-01 to 2027-02-28
Coverage Limit: $50,000
Deductible: $1,000

## Covered Perils
Sudden and accidental discharge of water from plumbing, heating or appliances; fire and
smoke; windstorm and hail; theft. Includes the cost of tearing out and replacing the part
of the building needed to reach the damaged system.

## Exclusions
Damage from water that leaked or seeped continuously over weeks or months, flood or surface
water, sewer backup unless the endorsement is purchased, wear and tear, and mould unless it
results directly from a covered sudden discharge.

## Claim Requirements
Take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, such as shutting off the water supply.
Provide photos, itemised contractor invoices and the date the damage was discovered.

## Additional Living Expenses
If the home is uninhabitable because of a covered loss, reasonable hotel and meal costs are
reimbursed for up to 30 days.
