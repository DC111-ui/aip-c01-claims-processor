# Personal Auto Policy

Policy Number: POL-100234
Policy Type: auto
Policyholder: Maria Okafor
Coverage Period: 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31
Coverage Limit: $25,000
Deductible: $500

## Covered Perils
Collision with another vehicle or object, comprehensive damage from theft, fire, hail,
falling objects and vandalism. Repairs are paid at the cost of an approved repair shop,
less the deductible.

## Exclusions
No cover for damage while racing or speed testing, while the vehicle is used for paid
rideshare or delivery work, damage caused intentionally, or while driven by an unlicensed
driver. Mechanical breakdown and normal wear and tear are not covered.

## Claim Requirements
Report the incident within 30 days. Collisions with an estimated repair cost above $2,000
require a police report number. Provide photos and at least one repair estimate.

## Rental Reimbursement
While the insured vehicle is being repaired for a covered loss, rental costs are reimbursed
up to $40 per day for a maximum of 14 days.
