# Single-Trip Travel Policy

Policy Number: POL-300552
Policy Type: travel
Policyholder: Priya Raman
Coverage Period: 2026-06-01 to 2026-09-30
Coverage Limit: $5,000
Deductible: $100

## Emergency Medical
Emergency medical and hospital treatment abroad is covered up to the policy limit. Keep
itemised bills and the treating doctor's report.

## Baggage and Personal Items
Lost, stolen or damaged baggage is covered up to $1,500 per trip, with a maximum of $500
for any single item. Electronics count as single items. Theft must be reported to local
police within 24 hours and a written police report provided.

## Trip Cancellation
Non-refundable trip costs are covered if the trip is cancelled because of the insured
person's illness or injury, confirmed by a doctor's note.

## Exclusions
Pre-existing medical conditions, travel to destinations with a government "do not travel"
advisory issued before booking, unattended baggage, and losses not reported to police.
